What House Hub does today

Everything below is built and works now, on phones and computers. Amounts are recorded in rand; landlords see a euro conversion next to them at the daily European Central Bank rate, or at an estimate you set yourself.

Money

  • Rent per tenantMonthly rent, due day, deposit and dated increases. A running statement shows what each tenant owes and when the next rent is due. Tenants report payments with proof; you confirm them.
  • Expense claimsTenants claim what they paid for (dog food, pool chlorine, electronics) with a photo of the receipt, split equally, by shares or by exact amounts. Anyone charged can dispute their share.
  • Who owes whomBalances across everyone, with the fewest payments needed to settle up.
  • Municipal and utility billsAttach the bill and split it equally, by weight, by days each person lived in the house, or by sub-meter usage.
  • Separate ledgersRunning totals kept apart from the house accounts, such as a car being paid off. Each can belong to one tenant, sync with a Google Sheet, and import or export Excel files. Tenants request entries with a receipt photo; you approve them.

The property

  • Maintenance calendarPlumbers, electricians, the pool service and vet visits, one-off or repeating weekly, fortnightly or monthly.
  • ContractorsEvery trade's details with tap-to-call and WhatsApp.
  • Pool and other assetsWater tests checked against target ranges, maintenance and issue logs with photos, and chemical stock that runs down as it's used. Garden, borehole, solar, and generator are ready to add.
  • Meter readingsElectricity and water readings with usage per day between readings.

Access

  • Invite by emailAdd a tenant's email and they're in when they sign in with Google or email and password.
  • Landlord and tenant rolesEnforced by the database itself, not just hidden buttons.
  • Private where it mattersA tenant sees only their own rent and ledgers, never anyone else's.
  • Works offlineRecent data stays on the phone, so the app opens instantly on patchy Wi-Fi.
